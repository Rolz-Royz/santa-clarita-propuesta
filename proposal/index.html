Your first website For Santa Clarita

A boutique that lives on Instagram and TikTok today deserves a home of its own, one that shows up on Google when a family searches for “quinceañera dresses in Utah”.

What we found

Google lists the shop as a “women’s clothing store”.

The Google profile has 5.0 stars, but the category is not “quinceañera dress shop” and the website link points to Instagram. With its own site and the right category, Google understands what the shop sells and who to show it to.

Yelp has no reviews yet.

The listing exists with address and hours, but no reviews. The site can invite happy customers to leave a review on Google and Yelp.

An old listing on the Ariana Vara store locator.

The Princesa by Ariana Vara store page shows “Santa Clarita” at 1500 W 3500 S with a different phone number. Worth asking them to update it to 2487 S Redwood Rd.

Several quince boutiques share West Valley City.

The Ariana Vara locator also lists Alicia’s Boutique, Luna Creations, VIP Quince and La Palomita in the same city. A site of its own, bilingual and built for phones, is how the shop stands out when a family compares options on Google. Review of competitors’ websites To confirm

The voice already exists: “Come & say yes to your dream dress”.

The site uses the shop’s own words, its pink, its crown and its gowns, and adds the Spotlight Vendor seal from the Once Upon a Princess expo (November 8, 2026).

What’s included

  • 8 pages in English
  • 8 pages in Spanish
  • Catalog by color
  • Buy or rent filter
  • “Build your look”
  • Book a fitting
  • Map, hours and “open now”
  • Call and directions always one tap away
  • ClothingStore structured data
  • Titles and descriptions per page
  • FAQ written for Google

What we need from you

Everything marked To confirm is information we chose not to make up.

  • Address and phone (Google and Yelp agree: 2487 S Redwood Rd · (801) 833-2708)
  • Hours, Tuesday to Sunday
  • WhatsApp number
  • Sale and rental prices (or “from” ranges)
  • How rental works: deposit, pick-up and return days
  • Size range and lead time to order in her size
  • Do you do alterations?
  • Appointment needed, or walk-ins welcome?
  • Charro suits, girls’ and baptism dresses: do you sell them?
  • Owner’s name and story (optional, but it helps a lot)
  • Families’ permission to use photos and names (for example, Nayeli’s XV)
  • Catalog photos of the new arrivals (ivory and lilac, and the gold “Belle” gown)
  • Domain: santaclaritaquince.com or another

Next steps

  1. We review the proposal and the facts to confirm together.
  2. Catalog photos of each gown (we can shoot them in the shop).
  3. Launch the site, fix the Google profile, and link it from Instagram and TikTok.
